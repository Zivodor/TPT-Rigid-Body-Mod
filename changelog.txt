Rigid Body Mod 0.0.3
New materials: ELAS (elastic, bounces back as high as it fell, grippy like rubber) and PELA (perfect elastic, frictionless).
New GRAB tool in the Tools menu: click and drag rigid bodies around, and let go to throw them.
Rigid bodies and lone rigid pixels now go through looping edges.
Simulation Options has a switch for rigid body air resistance.
Fast or spinning bodies no longer stop dead against each other instead of colliding.
Bounces come back at the right height: flat-sided bodies used to bounce too high.
Bodies no longer bump at the seams of floors and walls as they slide along them.
