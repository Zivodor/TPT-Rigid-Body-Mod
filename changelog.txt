Rigid Body Mod 0.0.1, first release.
Seven rigid materials in the Solids menu: RIGD (iron), RWOD (wood), RRBR (rubber), RICE (ice), RSTN (stone), RFOM (foam) and RGLD (gold).
Connected pixels of one material move as a single solid body: they fall, tip over, slide, bounce, stack and roll, simulated with Box2D.
Bodies float or sink in liquids by density, push liquid aside, and feel drag; hollow bodies trap air and float like boats.
Single pixels fall like powder. Molten rigid material is ordinary lava and turns back into a body when it sets.
Simulation Options has a switch for levelling liquid across floating bodies.
