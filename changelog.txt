Rigid Body Mod 0.0.2
Hollow bodies hold liquid: water inside a ball no longer leaks out as it bounces and rolls, even filled to the brim, and liquid around a body no longer gets in.
Liquid a body moves into is only ever pushed aside, never deleted; a body bulges by a pixel for a moment instead when there's no room.
Wind and air pressure now push rigid bodies, light ones (foam, wood) much more than heavy ones (stone, gold). Light bodies also fall more slowly through still air.
New PUSH tool in the Tools menu: click to shove rigid bodies, or draw a line (shift) to push along it, harder the longer it is.
